the whole introduction

I'm so happy you're here!

I'm Rachael, and I like to say that I want to build wonder and create joy. To me, this means I'm looking for everyday ways to bring happiness and fulfillment to the people who use the things I create. In everything I do, I want to serve others and bring a little light and delight through my relationships, skills, and talents.

I am a sophomore studying mechanical engineering and human-computer interaction at Carnegie Mellon, which is a long way of saying I like human-centered design, and making things people have to use, and thinking about how they will use it! I am most interested in interactive storytelling and enhancing the human experience through fun and functional technology.

I'm an engineer and designer interested in bridging the gap between how something works and how it feels to use. My background spans engineering, graphic design, and the performing arts, which has shaped how I think about user experience, storytelling, and form. Whether crafting a physical mechanism or a visual story, I'm driven by humanity at the center of it all.
